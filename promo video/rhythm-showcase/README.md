# Rhythm showcase revision

60 seconds at 1920×1080, 30 fps. Real editor footage and illustrative AI chat retained. Fixed crops with editorial cuts replace the previous floating/panning frames. Fast typography replaces drifting title cards.

## Audio

- Music: **Gimme that Groove!** by **Michael Ramir C.**, Mixkit, asset 872. New licensed stock track, not the previous generated score.
- Source: https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/funk/
- Asset: https://assets.mixkit.co/music/872/872.mp3
- License: Mixkit Stock Music Free License, https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree (accessed 2026-09-07). Source audio is not committed or offered as a standalone redistribution.
- Keyboard: project's `promo video/sfx/key_0.wav` through `key_7.wav`.
- Mouse: project's `promo video/remotion/public/sfx/mouse-click.wav`.
- No synthesized keystrokes, whooshes or confirmation dings. 91 recorded interaction events are mapped to the edit. Music ducks under interaction sounds.

Run `python3 mix.py`, copy mix.wav into remotion/public/motion-cut/rhythm.wav, then render the RhythmCut composition. The prepared shot videos from motion-showcase are required.
